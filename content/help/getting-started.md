## What is the Dyscalculia Knowledge Repository?

The Dyscalculia Knowledge Repository is a comprehensive, dynamic information system designed to support effective provision for learners with dyscalculia across the Ted Wragg Trust. It centralizes research, legal frameworks, assessment procedures, intervention strategies, case studies, and practical resources in one searchable location.

## Who is this repository for?

The repository serves multiple audiences:

- **SENCOs** - Assessment procedures, intervention strategies, case studies
- **Teachers** - Classroom adaptations, teaching strategies, practical resources
- **Leadership Teams** - Policy generation, compliance requirements, strategic oversight
- **Parents & Carers** - Understanding dyscalculia, home support strategies
- **Governors** - Statutory obligations, evidence-based practices, Trust-wide standards
- **External Specialists** - Collaborative resources and best practice sharing

## How do I navigate the repository?

### Main Navigation Sections

Click **"Explore the Toolkit"** to access eight core content areas:

1. **Legal & Policy Framework** ⚖️ - Statutory requirements and DfE guidance
2. **Dyscalculia Knowledge Base** 🧠 - Research and definitions
3. **Assessment Procedures** 📋 - Three-tier identification framework
4. **Interventions & Support** 🎯 - Evidence-based strategies
5. **Case Studies** 📚 - Real-world examples
6. **Resources & Tools** 🛠️ - Templates and materials
7. **Stakeholder Feedback** 💬 - Community input
8. **Change Log** 📝 - Version history

### Search Functionality

Use the search bar in the header to find specific content across all sections. Search results show:
- Document title and excerpt
- Section and category badges
- Direct links to full content

### Filters

Click the **Filters** button to refine content by:
- **Audience** - Role-specific content
- **Evidence Level** - Research quality indicators
- **Age Range** - Key stage or age appropriateness

## What are the Quick Links?

Quick Links provide shortcuts to key functions:

- **📄 View Policy Documents** - Access formatted policy outputs
- **➕ Submit Content** - Share expertise, case studies, or feedback
- **❓ Help & FAQs** - This section!

## How is content organized?

Each content item includes:

- **Title** - Clear, descriptive heading
- **Summary** - Brief overview
- **Metadata** - Category, audience, evidence level, review dates
- **Full Content** - Detailed information with citations
- **Tags** - Keywords for discovery

## What do the evidence level badges mean?

Content is tagged with evidence quality indicators:

- **Statutory Requirement** - Legal obligations from DfE guidance
- **High-Quality Evidence** - Peer-reviewed research, RCTs
- **Good Practice** - Evidence-informed approaches with strong support
- **Emerging Practice** - Promising approaches with limited evidence
- **Context-Specific** - Localized practices for Trust schools

## Can I download or print content?

Yes! Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) to print individual articles or save as PDF. Policy documents are pre-formatted for professional presentation.

## How often is content updated?

- **Review Cycle** - All content reviewed at least annually
- **Legislative Changes** - Updated immediately upon new statutory guidance
- **Emerging Research** - Quarterly updates from research partners
- **Practice Updates** - Continuous contributions from SENCOs and specialists

Review dates are shown on each content item.

## I can't find what I'm looking for. What should I do?

1. **Try alternative search terms** - Use synonyms or related concepts
2. **Browse by section** - Explore relevant categories manually
3. **Check filters** - Remove active filters that may be hiding content
4. **Submit a request** - Use the feedback form to suggest new content
5. **Contact support** - Email senco@tedwraggtrust.org for assistance

## How do I get help with a specific student?

This repository provides general guidance and evidence-based strategies. For individual student support:

1. **Consult your School SENCO** - First point of contact for individual cases
2. **Use assessment procedures** - Follow the three-tier identification framework
3. **Review relevant case studies** - Find similar scenarios and interventions
4. **Access external specialists** - Educational psychologists for diagnostic assessment
