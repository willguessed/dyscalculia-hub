## Who can submit content to the repository?

Anyone within the Ted Wragg Trust community or working with the Trust can submit content:

- **Staff members** - Teachers, SENCOs, TAs, leadership
- **External specialists** - Educational psychologists, specialist teachers
- **Research partners** - Universities, research organizations
- **Stakeholders** - Parents, governors (feedback category)

## What types of content can I submit?

You can submit content in **four categories**:

### 🎯 Interventions & Support
- Teaching strategies and classroom adaptations
- Reasonable adjustments that have worked
- Evidence-based intervention approaches
- Differentiation techniques

### 📚 Case Studies
- Real-world examples of identification processes
- Successful intervention implementations
- Collaborative working examples
- Progress monitoring outcomes

### 🛠️ Resources & Tools
- Assessment materials you've developed
- Lesson plans and teaching resources
- Templates and planning tools
- Training materials

### 💬 Feedback
- Insights from your practice
- Suggestions for repository improvements
- Parent or student perspectives
- External partnership feedback

## What content CANNOT be submitted through the form?

Content subject to higher authority must go through different channels:

- **Legal & Policy Documents** - Managed by MAT SEND Lead
- **Dyscalculia Knowledge Base (Research)** - Academic content from research partners
- **Assessment Procedures** - Standardized frameworks requiring specialist approval

Contact the MAT SEND Lead for these content types.

## What information do I need to provide?

**Required fields:**
- Content category and title
- Summary and full content
- Primary audience
- Your name, email, and role

**Optional but recommended:**
- Evidence level and source
- Tags for searchability
- Age range applicability
- Urgency indicator

## What happens after I submit content?

Your submission follows a **structured review process**:

### Stage 1: Submission Received
- Auto-generated tracking number (e.g., SUB-123456)
- Email confirmation sent to you
- Submission logged in review queue

### Stage 2: Initial Review (5-10 working days)
SENCO Lead or designated reviewer assesses:
- ✓ Accuracy and evidence alignment
- ✓ Clarity and accessibility
- ✓ Appropriate metadata and tagging
- ✓ No duplication of existing content

**Possible outcomes:**
- **Approved** - Proceeds to publication
- **Minor Revision** - Small edits needed (you'll be contacted)
- **Major Revision** - Significant changes required
- **Rejected** - Not suitable (with explanation)

### Stage 3: Stakeholder Consultation (If Required)
For significant updates or new policy-level content:
- Online survey or focus group consultation
- Minimum 2-week feedback period
- Your submission may be shared with affected parties
- Feedback synthesized and incorporated

### Stage 4: Leadership Approval (If Required)
Policy-level content requires:
- MAT SEND Lead approval
- Trust Board sign-off (for major policies)

Operational content approved by relevant school leader.

### Stage 5: Publication
- Content added to repository with publication date
- Automated notifications sent to relevant stakeholders
- Change log updated
- You receive publication confirmation

### Stage 6: Monitoring
- Review date set (typically 12 months)
- Community feedback mechanism active
- Annual comprehensive review

## How long does the review process take?

**Timeline estimates:**

| Content Type | Typical Review Time |
|---|---|
| Feedback, small resources | 5-7 working days |
| Interventions, case studies | 7-10 working days |
| Significant resources | 10-15 working days |
| Policy-level content | 4-6 weeks (includes consultation) |

**Urgent submissions** (flagged during form completion) receive priority review within 48 hours.

## Will I be notified about my submission status?

Yes! You'll receive email updates at key stages:

- ✉️ **Submission received** - Immediate confirmation
- ✉️ **Review in progress** - After 3 days
- ✉️ **Revision requested** - If changes needed
- ✉️ **Approved for publication** - Final decision
- ✉️ **Published** - Live on repository

## Can I track my submission?

Your **tracking ID** (e.g., SUB-123456) allows you to:
- Reference in follow-up emails
- Inquire about status
- Link related submissions

Keep this ID for your records.

## What if my submission is rejected?

Rejections are rare and always include:

- **Clear reasoning** - Why it wasn't suitable
- **Suggestions** - How to improve or reframe
- **Alternative routes** - Other ways to share the information

Common rejection reasons:
- Duplication of existing content
- Outside repository scope
- Insufficient evidence base
- Better suited to different channel (e.g., staff newsletter)

You're encouraged to **revise and resubmit** based on feedback.

## Can I submit anonymously?

No. All submissions require:
- Your name (for attribution and follow-up)
- Email address (for communication)
- Role (for context)

However, you can request that **published content** be attributed to:
- Your school (e.g., "Contributed by XYZ Primary")
- Your role (e.g., "Submitted by a SENCO in the Trust")
- Generic attribution (e.g., "Trust Contribution")

Indicate this preference in the submission form comments.

## What if I've made a mistake in my submission?

**Before publication:**
- Email senco@tedwraggtrust.org with your tracking ID
- Request edits or withdrawal
- Resubmit if needed

**After publication:**
- Use the repository feedback mechanism
- Content will be flagged for review
- Updates made in next review cycle

## Can I submit content on behalf of someone else?

Yes, but you must:
- Have their permission
- Credit them appropriately in the submission
- Provide their contact information for follow-up

Example: A SENCO submitting a teacher's successful intervention strategy.

## How is my intellectual property protected?

- **Attribution** - All content credited to original contributor
- **Trust use only** - Not republished externally without permission
- **Copyright** - You retain copyright; Trust has license to use
- **Commercial resources** - Cannot be submitted if subject to external copyright

By submitting, you grant the Trust a **non-exclusive license** to use the content within the repository for educational purposes.

## Can I update content I've previously submitted?

Yes! Once published:
- Use the feedback form to suggest updates
- Reference the original content title
- Provide updated information or resources

You'll be contacted to confirm the updates before they're published.

## What makes a good submission?

**High-quality submissions:**

✓ **Clear and specific** - Focused on one topic/strategy
✓ **Well-structured** - Headings, bullet points, logical flow
✓ **Evidence-based** - References research or practice evidence
✓ **Practical** - Actionable advice, real examples
✓ **Accessible** - Clear language, minimal jargon
✓ **Complete** - All relevant details provided

**Examples:**
- ❌ "This is a good intervention" (too vague)
- ✓ "Visual number line intervention: 10-week program for KS2 students with dyscalculia, showing 30% improvement in number bond fluency"

## Do I get recognition for my contribution?

Absolutely! Contributors receive:

- **Byline attribution** - Your name on the published content
- **Professional development** - Counts toward CPD hours
- **Community recognition** - Highlighted in Trust communications
- **Portfolio evidence** - Use in performance reviews, applications
- **Impact** - Your work helps learners across the Trust

Top contributors may be invited to:
- Present at Trust training days
- Co-author research publications
- Join working groups or advisory panels
